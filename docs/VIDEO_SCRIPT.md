## [0:30 – 1:45] THE DEMO

*[Switch to color. Show phone/tablet. App opens — SpeakUp logo.]*

VOICEOVER:
"Meet SpeakUp. A free, offline AI communication companion — powered by Gemma 4."

*[Show child profile creation — 10 seconds]*

"Create a profile for your child in seconds."

*[Show visual card grid — adult taps 'water' card]*

"Emma points at her cup and makes a soft sound."

*[Show gesture panel — select 'pointing']*
*[Show sound panel — select 'soft mmm']*
*[Open camera panel — capture a cup on the table. Gemma 4 detects "cup" and kitchen setting.]*

"We select her signals, and the camera frame becomes part of the same Gemma 4 reasoning step."

*[Hit big 'Interpret' button. Loading spinner. Then result appears.]*

"Gemma 4 — running completely on this device — interprets the signals."

*[Big result card: 'I want water, please.' Confidence: 94%]*
*[Phone speaks aloud: 'I want water, please.']*

"And speaks for her."

*[Caregiver taps 'That's right']*

"When we confirm — SpeakUp remembers. It learns Emma's personal communication language."

*[Show dashboard — '8 communications this week. 3 new patterns learned.']*

"Over time, accuracy improves. The AI adapts to this specific child — not a generic model."
