## Recommended Recording Flow
1. Record real app footage locally. Use QuickTime screen recording on macOS.
2. Follow the production plan and storyboard.
3. Import the recording into Canva.
4. Add the generated thumbnail, trims, captions, and light transitions.
5. Export as MP4 and upload to YouTube as public or unlisted.

Judges should see real working app footage, not only AI-generated visuals.
