SpeakUp is a local Gemma 4 communication companion for people who cannot always rely on speech.

It supports autistic, nonspeaking, minimally speaking, AAC, aphasia, and apraxia communication needs without sending private communication data to the cloud.

A caregiver starts on the About page, then opens Profiles. Each communicator has a separate memory, care plan, therapist details, and dedicated device mode.

In device mode, the communicator sees simple one-tap cards first. The Gemma chat stays visible, so the person can vocalize or select common signals without navigating through a complex app.

When Emma taps water, SpeakUp sends the signal plus Emma's profile memory to Gemma 4 running locally through Ollama. Gemma returns structured JSON, a confidence score, and a first-person phrase that can be spoken aloud.

The chat panel also uses memory. If Emma makes a soft sound, Gemma can connect it to earlier confirmed patterns and help the caregiver understand what may be happening.

Advanced communication tools are still available for caregivers: camera context, audio recording, gesture input, and signal chips. The communicator can view the camera or keep it hidden for sensory comfort.

The progress dashboard turns confirmed interactions into weekly patterns and summaries. The care-team page keeps therapist contact details and export-ready notes in one place.

SpeakUp is not a medical device or a replacement for clinicians. It is a privacy-first assistive communication support tool powered by Gemma 4, built to help more families be heard.
